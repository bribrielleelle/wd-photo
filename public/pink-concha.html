Classic Pink Strawberry Conchas

⏱ Prep: 2 hrs 30 mins 🔥 Bake: 20 mins 🍽 Yield: 10 conchas ✨ Level: Medium

Ultra-soft, sweet yeast brioche buns crowned with the traditional shell-scored pink vanilla-strawberry sugar crust.

Ingredients:

  • 3 1/2 cups (440g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet (7g) active dry yeast & 1/2 cup warm milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar, 3 large eggs, 1/2 cup softened butter
  • Pink Crust: 3/4 cup powdered sugar, 3/4 cup flour, 1/2 cup shortening
  • 2 tbsp freeze-dried strawberry powder (or 2 drops pink gel)

Baking Instructions:

  1. Bloom yeast in warm milk with 1 tbsp sugar for 8 minutes until foamy.
  2. Knead flour, eggs, sugar, butter, and yeast until smooth and elastic (8 mins). Let rise 2 hours.
  3. Cream shortening, powdered sugar, flour, and pink strawberry powder to form crust paste. Divide into 10 disks.
  4. Shape dough into 10 balls, top each with a flattened pink crust disk, and score shell lines with a paring knife.
  5. Rise 45 minutes, then bake at 350°F (175°C) for 18–20 minutes until golden.
💡 Pink Secret Tip: Use powdered freeze-dried strawberries in the crust paste! It adds bright berry flavor and an authentic blush pink color without thinning the topping dough.
